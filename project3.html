LIONEL MESSI

Lionel Messi is the most decorated footballer in history, defined by an unparalleled collection of team titles and individual records. At the international level, he anchored Argentina’s golden era by winning the 2022 FIFA World Cup, two consecutive Copa América titles (2021, 2024), and the 2008 Olympic Gold Medal. His club career is equally legendary, highlighted by four UEFA Champions League titles and ten La Liga championships with FC Barcelona, alongside domestic trophies with Paris Saint-Germain and Inter Miami CF. Individually, Messi stands entirely alone with a record eight Ballon d'Or awards, six European Golden Shoes, and the distinction of being the only player to win two FIFA World Cup Golden Balls. He also holds the staggering Guinness World Record for the most goals in a single calendar year, scoring 91 goals in 2012.
